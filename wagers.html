Reading the lines…

Lines are a market median, pulled on a schedule and cached, never fetched from your browser. A gap is the model total less the market total; under 2 points is agreement. The model total is Iron Tuna’s own expected points for the two clubs, fitted from the games that do carry a line.

Reading the props…

A lean is a gap of at least 5% between the model and the book’s posted line, not a recommendation. Every row here is a quoted market. Where no book has priced a player the line would be the game total applied to his own share — the same input the model uses — so there is nothing to disagree with and he is left off.

Reading the TD board…
