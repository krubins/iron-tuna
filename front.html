How it works

Three inputs, in this order. Every board on the site is built from them and nothing else.

1

The market

Game totals, spreads and player props, pulled on a schedule. A sportsbook has its own money behind a number and corrects it in public within minutes of news landing.

2

The consensus

Expert projections, blended toward the market where a book prices the player and left alone where none does. The gap between the two is printed, never hidden.

3

Your league

Scoring, roster slots and waiver budget, saved once in this browser with no account. Every board restates its numbers in your points instead of somebody else’s. Customize my league →

Iron Tuna prices every player against the betting market first and the consensus projections second, then restates the numbers at your league’s scoring. Projections are not guarantees.
