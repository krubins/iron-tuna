How it works

The longer version →

Anyone can publish a projection. Vegas has money on theirs.

The market

Game totals, spreads and player props, pulled on a schedule and converted into projected fantasy points, with a sportsbook’s own money and a paid modelling staff behind each number.

The consensus

Expert projections, blended toward the market where a book prices the player and left alone where none does; the gap between the two is printed, never hidden.

Your league

Scoring, roster slots and waiver budget, saved once in this browser with no account, so every board restates its numbers in your points.
