Auction Watch: June 28, 2026

Mike Raines·Usage and Opportunity Analyst·Iron Tuna

Today's auction-relevant signals, each with the player named and what it means for your bids. Verified from reputable reporting; values are projections, not guarantees.

DeVonta Smith inherits the WR1 role in Philadelphia

The Eagles traded A.J. Brown to the Patriots, leaving DeVonta Smith as the clear top target for Jalen Hurts for the first time since his rookie season. That vacated volume lands on a receiver who has already shown he can produce as a secondary option. Pay up a tier for Smith; his price still lags the role, and a true WR1 workload in a strong offense is worth chasing.

Discount Malik Nabers after another knee procedure

Malik Nabers is working back from a torn ACL and meniscus and underwent an April procedure to remove scar tissue, and the Giants responded by stacking the receiver room with veterans like JuJu Smith-Schuster and Odell Beckham Jr. The GM has said he expects Nabers fine for Week 1, but the signings signal real risk. Build the injury risk into your bid and do not pay an elite WR price for a player who may start the year limited.

Pay up for Jahmyr Gibbs in Detroit's new offense

New Lions offensive coordinator Drew Petzing comes from an Arizona scheme that fed running backs heavily in the passing game, and Detroit is leaning on Jahmyr Gibbs as the every down feature back. A back this explosive with both rushing and receiving volume carries a rare floor and ceiling combination. Pay up; the projected pass game usage in a productive offense makes Gibbs one of the safest expensive bids on the board.

Washington's backfield is a committee

Jacory Croskey-Merritt is positioned for the early down and lead back work in Washington after running for 805 yards and 8 touchdowns as a first year back, while free agent addition Rachaad White slots in as the passing down option. This looks like a true committee. Target Croskey-Merritt as the higher upside piece and treat White as a cheap PPR dart rather than bidding either up as a workhorse.

Jonathon Brooks is a cheap Carolina dart

Cleared from his ACL recovery, Jonathon Brooks has climbed about a round and a half in ADP and profiles as a low end RB3 behind Chuba Hubbard in Carolina. He carries real standalone and contingent value at a late round cost. Grab him as a cheap dart who could vault into a lead role if Hubbard misses time.
