Getting started

Is Iron Tuna a draft tool or an in-season tool?

Both, and in-season is where the season is. Every week Iron Tuna publishes rankings and projections re-scored at your league’s settings, start/sit and rest-of-season reads, waiver and FAAB prices, trade analysis, a DFS slate with salary-based value boards and a lineup builder, and the betting-market comparisons the rest of it is built on. The draft tools — the auction cheat sheet, the live Auction Manager, the snake board and the AI Value Coach — are all still here and still work; they simply matter most in July and August.

Do I need an account?

No. The free boards need no signup, and your saved league lives in your own browser. An account only exists so a purchase and your league follow you to another device.

Free and paid

What do I get without paying anything?

Most of the site. Weekly rankings, waiver and FAAB prices, the trade finder, the DFS boards, Game Intel, Vegas Edge, the desk’s written coverage and the full draft board at expert default values are all free, with no signup.

How much does the paid unlock cost, and what does it add?

A one-time $9.99 for the current season, with no subscription. It unlocks values recalculated to your league’s exact custom scoring, a reorderable board, the live Draft Day tools and the AI Value Coach, plus the premium insight set.

Your league

Can I use my exact league scoring?

Yes. Set your teams, roster slots, scoring and FAAB budget once and every board on the site restates its numbers in your points instead of somebody else’s — including superflex and 2-QB. Saving your league is free and lives in your browser; the $9.99 unlock adds full custom scoring inside the draft app.

Can Iron Tuna read my actual league?

You can connect a Sleeper or Yahoo league and Iron Tuna will import your scoring, rosters, matchups and free-agent pool, then personalize the rankings, lineup, waiver and trade guidance to them. You authorize it yourself, we ask for read-only access, and you can disconnect at any time on My Leagues — which deletes the imported league data.

Trust

Does Iron Tuna take bets or run contests?

No. Iron Tuna publishes information and analysis. It does not accept wagers, hold funds, or operate a sportsbook, an exchange, a daily fantasy contest or a prediction market. Odds and salaries shown are informational and may differ at the venue. You are responsible for the laws that apply where you are.

Is any of this written by AI?

Some of it, and it is labeled where it is. The analyst desk, parts of the daily editorial coverage and the Value Coach are model-generated from the same data the boards price from. The projections, rankings, prices and market numbers are computed, not written.

Draft

Can I use it during a live draft?

Yes. The draft board runs in your browser and keeps working even if your Wi-Fi drops mid-draft.

What is the Value Coach?

An assistant that reads your actual draft against your roster and your league and calls the move that wins you the most points: who to target, what they are worth, and when to pivot. You can ask it anything mid-draft.
