Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Iron Tuna, the fantasy football auction toolkit.

Is Iron Tuna only for auction drafts?

Auction is what it is built for, and it is the only thing on the site that gets the full treatment: live max-bid maths, budget tracking across every team in the room, and a price on every player for your exact settings. Snake is supported too — a best-available board with survival odds for your next pick — and you can switch to it from the ribbon on the home page.

Do I need an account?

No. Build your free, custom cheat sheet with no signup. An account just lets you save your league and reopen it on draft day.

Can I use my exact league scoring?

Yes — that is the heart of the $9.99 unlock. Set your scoring, roster slots, and league size, and every value recalculates to your exact settings, including superflex and 2-QB. The free board uses expert default scoring so you can try it first.

How much does it cost?

The board with expert default values is free in every format. A one-time $9.99 unlocks your league's custom scoring and values, a reorderable board, the AI Value Coach, and the live Draft Day tools.

Can I use it during the live draft?

Yes. It is built to run live and keeps working even if your Wi-Fi drops mid-draft.

What is Value Coach?

It advises you on every pick, reading your exact draft against your roster and your league to call the move that wins you the most points: who to target, what they are worth, and when to pivot. You can also ask it anything mid-draft.
