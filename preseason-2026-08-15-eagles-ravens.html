Ravens 24, Eagles 7: Philadelphia goes 0 for 9 on third down, and Baltimore's rookies put up 440 yards

August 15, 2026 · M&T Bank Stadium, Baltimore, MD

Baltimore beat Philadelphia 24 to 7 in front of 68,868 at M&T Bank Stadium, outgaining the Eagles 440 to 159. Philadelphia did not convert a single third down in nine attempts, which is difficult to do on purpose.

The Eagles offense produced nothing across three quarterbacks

Tanner McKee went 6 of 10 for 35 and took a sack. Andy Dalton managed 3 of 6 for 20. Rookie Cole Payton was the only one who moved the ball, going 4 of 9 for 37 with a touchdown while running 3 times for 45 yards. Philadelphia threw for 83 yards as a team and finished 0 for 9 on third down. None of the players you draft in Philadelphia were involved, so this does not move a price, but a backup unit this inert is worth remembering if you are counting on Eagles depth.

Joe Fagnano handled a near starter's workload cleanly

The Baltimore rookie completed 22 of 28 for 224 with a touchdown and an interception, a 97.0 rating, and took no sacks. Tyler Huntley followed at 6 of 7 for 76. Baltimore threw for 298 yards on the night. Twenty eight attempts is a real preseason sample and Fagnano handled it, which matters mostly for the roster spot at the back of the quarterback room rather than for anyone's auction board.

Baltimore's rookie pass catchers outplayed everyone on the field

Elijah Sarratt caught 6 of 7 for 66, Matthew Hibner 5 of 5 for 61 with a 29 yard long, and Ja'Kobi Lane 3 of 3 for 38 with the game's first touchdown. That is 14 catches on 15 targets and 165 yards from three rookies. Baltimore's established pass catchers were not out there, so none of this displaces anybody you are drafting. It is a depth chart forming behind the starters, and Sarratt is the one with both the volume and the catch rate.

Three Baltimore rookie backs split 31 carries almost evenly

Adam Randall took 12 for 46 and a 4 yard touchdown, Elijah Tau-Tolliver 10 for 42, and Dontae McMillan 9 for 40. Baltimore ran for 142 yards without anyone separating. Even splits this deep into the preseason usually mean the coaching staff has not decided, or has already decided and is evaluating the others. Either way there is no cheap Baltimore back to buy out of this game.

The Eagles injury report is thin but touches the receiver room twice

Elijah Moore is questionable with a neck injury and rookie Makai Lemon is questionable with a hamstring. Will Shipley led the Philadelphia rushing with 22 yards on 7 carries and Tank Bigsby got 2 carries for 5. Hollywood Brown caught his only target for 4 yards. Two questionable receivers plus an offense that gained 159 yards is not a reason to move a Philadelphia starter's price, but it is a reason not to reach for the second tier of their pass catchers.
