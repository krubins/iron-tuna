Preseason Week 1

Bengals 16, Lions 14: Joe Burrow takes six snaps, Luke Altmyer takes the beating, and Detroit's backfield gets interesting

August 13, 2026 · Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati, OH

Cincinnati beat Detroit 16 to 14 in front of 62,795 at Paycor Stadium, and the scoreboard is the least informative thing about it. Four quarterbacks played for the Bengals and the one who matters threw six passes.

Joe Burrow's cameo tells you nothing, and that is the point

Burrow went 5 of 6 for 39 yards and left. Josh Johnson took the largest share at 10 of 19 for 84, Joe Flacco threw the game's only touchdown pass, and Sean Clifford mopped up. Cincinnati's first team offense was on the field for a handful of snaps and produced nothing you can extrapolate from. Burrow's auction price does not move on six attempts, in either direction, and anyone telling you it should is selling something.

Ja'Marr Chase caught one pass and that is a non event

Chase saw a single target, caught it for 16 yards, and was done. Andrei Iosivas added 2 for 20. The receiver reps went to Dohnte Meyers, 3 for 29, and rookie Colbie Young, 2 for 28 on 4 targets. Preseason target share at the top of a depth chart is noise. Chase stays exactly where he was on your board, at the top, at full price.

Luke Altmyer had a rough first extended look

The Detroit rookie went 13 of 22 for 130 with a touchdown and 2 interceptions, a 53.2 rating, and took a sack for 12. Russ Yeast and Jordan Battle picked him off. He did convert the game's last score, a 24 yard touchdown to Tarik Black plus a two point run he kept himself. Joshua Dobbs went 4 of 8 for 32 behind him. Detroit turned it over 3 times and converted 4 of 11 on third down.

Jacob Saylors and Raheem Blackshear both ripped off long runs

Saylors carried 8 times for 55 with a 27 yard long and caught 3 of 5 targets for 28. Blackshear went 5 for 33 with a 24 yard long. Kye Robichaux got 8 carries for 31. Detroit ran for 129 yards as a team on a night its passing game was a mess. Neither back is rosterable in a standard league today. Both are worth a note if the Detroit depth chart thins out, and Saylors is the one who caught the ball.

Sam LaPorta's hip is the Detroit designation to watch

LaPorta is listed questionable with a hip injury and did not feature here. Cincinnati's list is longer but shallower in fantasy terms, with Dax Hill, Bryan Cook, Shemar Stewart and Myles Murphy all questionable, none of them offensive pieces. A questionable tag in mid August on a tight end who is already drafted as a starter is a hold, not a discount. Do not pay up for the backup.
