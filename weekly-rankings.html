How to read the two columns

Fantasy Consensus is the projection consensus, scored at the setting you choose and nudged by a player’s live usage once three games have earned it. Betting Odds is the same player priced off the sportsbook: his own posted props where a book has quoted them, otherwise the posted game line’s scoring environment applied to his line, otherwise a fitted team rating for a fixture nobody has posted yet. The Gap column is the second minus the first, in points and in rank slots, and the verdict beside it is the site’s standing classification of that gap. Neither column is a tip. They are two honest readings of the same player, and the argument between them is the useful part.
