Preseason Week 1

Bears 34, Browns 10: Deshaun Watson outplays both young quarterbacks, and Chicago hangs 348 through the air

August 15, 2026 · Soldier Field, Chicago, IL

Chicago beat Cleveland 34 to 10 in front of 43,537 at Soldier Field, throwing for 348 yards against a Browns defense that gave up three touchdown passes. Cleveland used three quarterbacks and the oldest one was comfortably the best.

The Cleveland quarterback competition went badly for the young arms

Deshaun Watson completed 11 of 15 for 126 at a 98.2 rating. Shedeur Sanders went 6 of 11 for 79 with an interception, a 39.6 rating. Dillon Gabriel managed 5 of 8 for 23 with a pick and 2 sacks taken for 17 yards, a 27.1 rating. Cleveland turned it over three times. Whatever you believed about how this room would sort itself out, this game pushed against the young quarterbacks, and that is a reason to keep every Browns pass catcher cheap rather than to bet on a breakout.

Quinshon Judkins is questionable and did nothing when he played

Judkins carried 4 times for 9 yards and now carries an undisclosed questionable designation. Rookie Davon Booth was the most productive back on the field for either team, 4 carries for 53 with a 28 yard run. Dylan Sampson gained 6 yards on 4 carries but caught 2 passes for 43 including a 43 yard gain. If Judkins is your projected Cleveland starter, this is a soft warning rather than an alarm, and Booth is the cheap name to remember.

KC Concepcion scored on the ground and led the rookie receivers

Concepcion took a jet sweep 14 yards for Cleveland's only touchdown and added 3 catches for 27 on 4 targets. Gage Larvadain saw a team high 7 targets for 4 catches and 37 yards. Jerry Jeudy played briefly, catching both his targets for 18. With Cedric Tillman questionable and Jamari Thrash on injured reserve, the Browns receiver room has openings, and Concepcion is the rookie using them. Minimum bid in deeper formats.

Case Keenum was nearly perfect and it means nothing for anyone's price

Keenum went 9 of 10 for 151 with 2 touchdowns and a 158.3 rating. Tyson Bagent started, threw for 169 with a touchdown and a pick, and is now listed questionable with a hamstring. Rookie Miller Moss finished at 3 of 8. Chicago's 430 yards came against progressively deeper Cleveland units. Do not price a backup quarterback off a 158.3 rating in the third quarter of a preseason game.

Salvon Ahmed turned three touches into 72 yards and a score

Ahmed caught 3 of 3 for 55 including a 49 yard touchdown and added 17 rushing yards on 2 carries. Kaden Davis led all receivers with 4 catches for 88 and a 41 yard long. Roschon Johnson took the most carries at 8 for 34. Chicago's skill group produced four different receiving touchdown threats and no clear pecking order behind the starters. Nobody here is worth a bid above the minimum, and Ahmed is the one whose usage would actually translate if an injury opened a role.
