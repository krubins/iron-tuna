Preseason Week 1

Chargers 27, Texans 7: Amar Johnson scores three ways, and Houston's receiver room takes a hit it cannot absorb

August 13, 2026 · NRG Stadium, Houston, TX

Los Angeles beat Houston 27 to 7 in front of 67,655, outgaining the Texans 379 to 209. One running back on the roster bubble had the best night of anyone on the field, and one receiver who was not on it had the most consequential.

Jayden Higgins is doubtful with a knee injury the feed lists as an ACL

That is the most expensive line in this box score and Higgins never took a snap in it. A doubtful designation attached to an ACL points one direction. Until Houston says otherwise, take Higgins off your board rather than hunting a discount, and move his projected target share to the names still standing. Jaylin Noel, also listed questionable with a hamstring, is the obvious beneficiary if he gets right, which makes him a buy at a price that has not caught up yet.

Amar Johnson made the roster case of the night

Johnson ran 6 times for 17 yards with 2 touchdowns, converted a two point run, and caught both his targets for 68 yards including a 55 yard gain. That is three different ways to score points in one preseason game from a back who was not in the conversation. Kimani Vidal managed 8 yards on 5 carries and rookie Greg Desrosiers Jr. got the most carries with 10 for 27. Johnson is a roster bubble player who just made himself hard to cut. Watch the Chargers backfield, do not bid on it yet.

Trey Lance took the first team work and was uneven

Lance went 12 of 20 for 164 with an interception and took 2 sacks, a 65.4 rating, while adding 27 yards on 3 carries. DJ Uiagalelei was sharper in relief at 6 of 9 for 120 and a touchdown. The Chargers turned it over twice and still won by 20 because Houston could not move. Neither quarterback is a fantasy asset.

Houston's offense produced 209 yards and one scoring drive

Davis Mills went 9 of 15 for 82 with a 5 yard touchdown to Jared Wayne. Graham Mertz added 4 of 7 for 23. Rookie Noah Whittington led the rushing with 13 carries for 40 yards at 3.1 a pop. Rookie tight end Marlin Klein caught his only target for 31, the longest Houston play of the night. With Higgins doubtful and Brevin Jordan questionable with a knee, the Texans pass catching depth is being tested in August, and none of the names filling in have earned a bid.

The Chargers offensive line report is worth a glance before you buy the skill players

Tyler Biadasz is out with a knee injury, Rashawn Slater is questionable, and rookie Isaiah World is out with a knee. Quentin Johnston is separately listed questionable with a lower body injury. None of those are fantasy players themselves, but three offensive line designations at once is the kind of thing that quietly caps a running back's ceiling. It is a reason to shade a dollar or two off a Chargers skill position bid, not to avoid the team.
