Auction Watch: July 20, 2026

Today's auction-relevant signals, each with the player named and what it means for your bids. Verified from reputable reporting; values are projections, not guarantees.

Mitchell camp riser

No player has climbed further in Underdog ADP over the past month than Jets receiver Adonai Mitchell, up from pick 18.02 to 15.05 on steady camp praise from head coach Aaron Glenn and quarterback Geno Smith. In his first full season in New York after the midseason Sauce Gardner trade, the buzz keeps stacking. His auction price is still rising, so buy in now before August drafts get pricier.

Nabers slow ramp

Malik Nabers opens Giants camp on the physically unable to perform list after two procedures on his right knee, with veterans reporting July 28. Beat reporting points to a slow ramp toward a Week 3 debut on a pitch count rather than a full Week 1 workload. Bid only at a clear discount and plan around a quiet September.

Judkins cheap RB2

Quinshon Judkins is cutting and getting upfield in practice after the dislocated ankle and fractured fibula that ended his rookie year, and he profiles as the Browns lead back. At a sixth round ADP he is a cheap bet on RB2 volume. Keep bids modest, though; Cleveland projects as one of the weakest scoring offenses, which caps his touchdown ceiling.

Diggs wait and see

Stefon Diggs remains unsigned with camps opening within days, and he is the biggest recent faller in the draftable range. He caught 85 passes for 1,013 yards in New England last year, but at 32 with no team, his fantasy value is frozen. Spend nothing beyond a $1 dart until he signs; a landing spot could change his price overnight.

Update, August 19: resolved. Diggs signed a one-year deal worth up to $12 million with Washington on August 5; a modest WR3 price now applies.

Kamara committee

Alvin Kamara agreed to a restructured deal on July 15 that keeps him in New Orleans for 2026, ending the offseason uncertainty. He is expected to split the backfield with Travis Etienne Jr., who signed a four-year deal this offseason. Treat Kamara as a cheap flex only, and shave a few dollars off Etienne for the committee risk.

Skattebo value

Cam Skattebo's rehab from the ankle fracture that ended his 2025 season has drawn consistently positive reports, and he is expected to practice early in Giants camp with no real risk of missing Week 1. If your room still prices in the injury, take the discount while it lasts.
