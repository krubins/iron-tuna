# Working agreements

## Response format

Lead with the point, and keep it short.

- Open with the answer, decision, or result in the first sentence. No preamble,
  no restating the question, no "Great question" or "Let me explain."
- Give only the support the reader needs to act on it. Cut background,
  caveats, and alternatives unless they change what the reader should do.
- Prefer short sentences, short paragraphs, and plain words. Use a list only
  when the items are genuinely parallel.
- Do not narrate your process ("First I looked at..."). Report what you found.
- If a question is ambiguous, answer the most likely reading, then state the
  assumption in one line.

When a response runs long, close it with an **Executive Summary** at the end.

- "Long" means anything past a few short paragraphs: multi-file investigations,
  design write-ups, review findings, migration notes, or any answer with
  headings, tables, or a numbered list of findings.
- Put the summary last, not first, and label it `## Executive summary`.
- Keep it to 3-6 bullets or one tight paragraph: what was asked, what was found
  or changed, and what needs a decision or a next step.
- Short answers (a one-line fact, a single file path, a yes/no) need no summary.
  Do not pad a short answer to earn one.
