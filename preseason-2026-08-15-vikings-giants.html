Preseason Week 1

Vikings 13, Giants 10: Four quarterbacks for Minnesota, and Malik Nabers is listed with an ACL

August 15, 2026 · MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ

Minnesota beat the Giants 13 to 10 at MetLife Stadium in front of the largest crowd of the preseason so far. Four different quarterbacks threw a pass for the Vikings. The most important player in the building did not play at all.

Malik Nabers is questionable with a knee injury the feed lists as an ACL

He did not appear. That designation on that player is the single most valuable piece of information in this box score, and it is worth confirming against a team statement before you act, because questionable and ACL rarely belong in the same line. If it is real, Nabers comes off your board and the Giants passing game loses the only pass catcher on it worth a double digit bid. If it is a stale or precautionary tag, he is the biggest discount available. Do not guess in either direction, check before you bid.

Minnesota gave snaps to four quarterbacks and the order was not obvious

Carson Wentz went 9 of 14 for 81 with a touchdown and a 103.6 rating, the largest workload. J.J. McCarthy took 7 dropbacks and completed 4 for 34. Kyler Murray threw 3 passes. Max Brosmer threw 2 and is listed questionable with a thumb injury. A four man rotation in the middle preseason game is a coaching staff still deciding something. Until that resolves, treat every Minnesota pass catcher as carrying quarterback risk and bid accordingly.

Jordan Addison saw one target and Myles Price scored the touchdown

Addison caught his lone target for 8 yards. Price led the team with 4 catches for 51 on 4 targets, including the 1 yard touchdown from Wentz that decided it. Minnesota threw for 127 yards as a team. There is nothing here that changes Addison's price, which is set by his role in September, not his August target count. Price is a camp body playing well, which is the most common and least valuable thing in preseason football.

Jaxson Dart was efficient and got hit, Brandon Allen was neither

Dart went 3 of 4 for 26 with a 15 yard touchdown to rookie Malachi Fields, a 131.2 rating, but took 2 sacks for 15 yards on four dropbacks. Jameis Winston was the most productive Giant at 8 of 9 for 89. Brandon Allen threw 2 interceptions on 8 attempts. New York gained 201 yards and turned it over twice. The sack rate behind Dart is the detail worth carrying forward, because it caps the ceiling on every Giants skill player if it holds.

The Minnesota backfield split three ways and nobody claimed it

Aaron Jones Sr. carried 3 times for 27, Jordan Mason 3 for 17, and rookie Demond Claiborne took the bulk work with 12 carries for 34 at 2.8 a carry. On the Giants side Cam Skattebo went 4 for 19, Tyrone Tracy Jr. 2 for 5, and Devin Singletary caught a 40 yard pass on his only target. Neither backfield produced a starter's workload or a starter's efficiency. Both remain committees, and committees are where auction dollars go to die.
