Where these numbers come from

Iron Tuna reads the betting market first and the consensus projection feeds second. A sportsbook posts a number with its own money behind it and corrects it in public within minutes of news; a ranking carries no such penalty. When a priced market and an unpriced list disagree, the disagreement is the thing worth reading.

The week, the game states and the byes on this page come from the NFL schedule itself, not from the calendar. Game lines and totals are the market’s, pulled on a schedule and cached, never fetched from your browser. Where a number is derived rather than quoted, the page says so. Anytime-touchdown percentages without a priced prop are Poisson on the blended line and are labelled derived.
