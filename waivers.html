In-Season · Waivers

A ranking tells you who to want. FAAB is about what you pay.

Every other waiver tool ranks the wire. In a FAAB league a dollar under the winner buys nothing at all, so the price is the entire decision, and it is a decision about the eleven other budgets in your league rather than about the player.

The FAAB Advisor

Connect a Sleeper league and every free agent gets three numbers: the going rate computed one rival at a time from their remaining budget and their actual hole at the position, what he is worth to your roster over the weeks left, and what you should bid.

Open the FAAB Advisor

A flat wire correctly produces an empty table and the advice to save the budget. That is the answer no ranking will ever give you.

Where these numbers come from

Iron Tuna prices players against the betting market first and the consensus projection feeds second. A sportsbook posts a number with its own money behind it and corrects it in public within minutes of news; a ranking carries no such penalty. When a priced market and an unpriced list disagree, the disagreement is the thing worth reading.

The week, the game states and the byes on this page come from the NFL schedule itself, not from the calendar: a week is current until its own last game has finished. Game lines and totals are the market’s, pulled on a schedule and cached, never fetched from your browser. Where a number is derived rather than quoted, the page says so.
