In-Season · Stats

What has actually been played

Every other board on this site is a forecast. This one is not. It is the season as it has been played — the weekly stat lines and snap counts, accumulated week by week and scored at the setting you choose. Nothing here is modeled, blended or shrunk, and no odds touch it.

Reading the stat lines…

Why a stats page sits beside the rankings

A ranking is an argument about the weeks ahead. A stat line is a record of the weeks behind. They answer different questions, and the mistake worth avoiding is treating either as the other: a receiver who has scored on a quarter of his catches has a real number on this page and an unrepeatable one, and a back with sixty carries and no touchdowns has the opposite problem. Read this page for what a player has been given — targets, carries, snaps — and read the rankings for what he is likely to do with it next.

Where the numbers come from

Weekly stat lines and snap counts come from nflverse, refreshed after each week’s games are final, and are accumulated here week by week. Points are computed at the scoring you pick rather than stored, so the same underlying line serves a standard league and a full-PPR one. A player whose season line has not been published yet shows a dash rather than a zero, because those two say very different things.
