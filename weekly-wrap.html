How the wrap is written

The recap comes first

A game’s recap is written when the feed marks it final, and it leads the front page until the next game ends.

Three layers, every time

What scored, the targets and carries under it, and the usage that has not turned into points yet.

The summary is the story’s own

Each wrap is the recap’s closing section, so the short form cannot say something the long form does not.

Missing is missing

A game with no recap yet says so and says why, rather than showing an empty card.
