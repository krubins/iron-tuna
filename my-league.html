Your league, on every page that prints a number

A dollar figure means nothing until you know the budget behind it, and a rank means nothing until you know the scoring. Set yours once and every page on this site restates its numbers in them.

Sync my league

Connect the league you actually play in and Iron Tuna imports its exact scoring, every roster, the free-agent pool, your opponent and the standings. Rankings, the Pickup Advisor, Best Lineup, the Trade Finder and the stories then read your league instead of a default one. Nothing is re-typed.

What Iron Tuna imports: league settings and scoring, team names and manager display names, rosters, matchups, standings and transactions, from the provider you choose. Sleeper leagues are read by league ID from Sleeper’s public API. Yahoo leagues are read after you authorize Iron Tuna on Yahoo’s own site: Iron Tuna never sees your Yahoo password, asks for read-only access, and encrypts the tokens Yahoo issues. The data is used only to personalize your guidance and is never shown to unrelated users. Disconnecting a league stops syncing and deletes the imported data. Full details in the privacy policy.

Your in-season settings

Platform, scoring, teams and FAAB budget. Every board in the In-Season section — the weekly rankings, the waiver prices, the DFS values and the market comparisons — restates its numbers at these.

Free, no signup. Your league lives in your own browser and nowhere else: nothing on this page is uploaded, and clearing your browser storage removes it. That is the trade for not asking you for an account.

The FAAB budget is the one number most tools guess at. A $23 bid out of $100 and a $23 bid out of $1,000 are not the same decision, and a waiver price quoted without the budget behind it is not a price.

Where these numbers come from

Iron Tuna prices players against the betting market first and the consensus projection feeds second. A sportsbook posts a number with its own money behind it and corrects it in public within minutes of news; a ranking carries no such penalty. When a priced market and an unpriced list disagree, the disagreement is the thing worth reading.

The week, the game states and the byes on this page come from the NFL schedule itself, not from the calendar: a week is current until its own last game has finished. Game lines and totals are the market’s, pulled on a schedule and cached, never fetched from your browser. Where a number is derived rather than quoted, the page says so.
