What Iron Tuna imports, and what it never asks for

League settings and scoring, team names and manager display names, rosters, matchups, standings and transactions, from the provider you choose. Sleeper leagues are read by league ID from Sleeper’s public API. Yahoo leagues are read after you authorize Iron Tuna on Yahoo’s own site. CBS browser import reads settings, team names and rosters without copying credentials; refresh it using the extension. The separate CBS API connector uses a league token you provide. Iron Tuna never asks for a Yahoo or CBS password and encrypts provider tokens at rest. The data is used only to personalize your guidance and is never shown to unrelated users. Disconnecting a league stops syncing and deletes the imported data and that CBS league’s token. Full details in the privacy policy.

02

Your in-season settings

Platform, scoring, lineup, teams and FAAB budget. Every board in the In-Season section restates its numbers at these, whether or not a league is connected. Copy them in from a screenshot of your settings page, a paste of it, or the cheat sheet you already built — the same way the draft room asks — or set them by hand. The FAAB budget is the one number most tools guess at: a $23 bid out of $100 and a $23 bid out of $1,000 are not the same decision.

What the draft app knows

Your cheat sheet carries the same full scoring — every yardage divisor and touchdown value — and it is what the draft board prices against. The form on the left can copy it across rather than asking you for it twice.

Iron Tuna prices players against the betting market first and the consensus projection feeds second. A sportsbook posts a number with its own money behind it and corrects it in public within minutes of news; a ranking carries no such penalty. When a priced market and an unpriced list disagree, the disagreement is the thing worth reading.

The week, the game states and the byes on this page come from the NFL schedule itself, not from the calendar: a week is current until its own last game has finished. Game lines and totals are the market’s, pulled on a schedule and cached, never fetched from your browser. Where a number is derived rather than quoted, the page says so.
