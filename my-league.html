How many teams?

The size of the room sets what a replacement player is worth, and every value on the site is measured against that line. A twelve-team league and an eight-team league price the same running back differently.

The cheat sheet

The draft board carries the same scoring, down to every yardage divisor. Where you built one in this browser, this page opens already filled in from it rather than asking twice.

Your rosters

Who is on each team is a different question from how the league scores. My Leagues saves your league and reads every roster off one screenshot; the FAAB budget is set there and on the waiver tools.
