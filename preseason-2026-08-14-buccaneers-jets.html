Preseason Week 1

Buccaneers 24, Jets 16: Braelon Allen goes 31 yards on his first touch, and four Tampa Bay pass catchers land on the report

August 14, 2026 · MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ

Tampa Bay beat the Jets 24 to 16 in front of 62,082 at MetLife Stadium, scoring a third of its points on a 47 yard interception return. Neither team's starting offense stayed on the field long enough to matter, so look at who was not on it.

Four Tampa Bay pass catchers are listed with injuries at the same time

Emeka Egbuka is questionable with a toe, Jalen McMillan questionable with a knee, Tez Johnson questionable with a groin, and Cade Otton questionable with an undisclosed issue. None of them played. That is the entire projected receiving depth chart carrying August designations simultaneously. No single one of those is scary on its own. Together they are a reason to discount every Tampa Bay pass catcher by a dollar or two and to make sure you are not the person who pays up for the last healthy one.

Braelon Allen out produced Breece Hall on a quarter of the touches

Allen took 4 carries for 44 yards and a 31 yard touchdown, averaging 11.0. Hall also carried 4 times and gained 16. That is a tiny sample and Hall remains the more expensive player for good reasons, but Allen has been the more explosive of the two in these looks before. If Hall's price in your room reflects a bell cow workload, this is a small argument that it should not, and Allen at a few dollars is the correct hedge rather than a reach.

The Jets quarterback room did not settle anything

Brady Cook went 9 of 15 for 108, rookie Cade Klubnik added 5 of 7 for 56, and Bailey Zappe threw 2 interceptions on 10 attempts at a 35.4 rating. New York turned it over twice and gave up a pick six. Garrett Wilson caught 2 of 3 for 32 and left. Wilson's valuation is a bet on talent surviving the quarterback play, and nothing here made that bet safer or cheaper.

Jalon Daniels handled the Tampa Bay offense competently

The rookie went 10 of 15 for 111 without a turnover and ran in a 1 yard touchdown, an 88.5 rating. Connor Bazelak was less effective at 12 of 20 for 72. Garrett Greene led the receivers with 4 catches for 54 including a 35 yard gain, and rookie Dean Patterson caught 5 of 7 for 24. These are roster bubble players competing for the last two or three spots, not fantasy assets.

Josh Williams is the Tampa Bay back who scored, and it still means little

Williams took 8 carries for 27 yards and a 12 yard touchdown. Sean Tucker got 4 for 12. Rookie Kadarius Calloway carried 8 times for 13 and is now on injured reserve. Tampa Bay ran for 69 yards as a team on 25 attempts. Nobody in this group did anything to earn a bid, and the touchdown is the kind of preseason stat that makes a player look rosterable when the yards per carry says otherwise.
